This repository holds the working record of an architect who moved from designing shelter after floods to asking what our maps, datasets and models can — and cannot — say about the people who live with flood risk. Each item is catalogued by place, source and evidential status.

The artifacts below are fragments drawn from fieldwork, spatial analysis and experiments with human-centred AI. Drag them, shuffle them, or select any item to read its label. Where evidence runs out, the record says so: unobserved, unsupported, or unanswerable are kept as first-class entries.

M.Arch thesis · NUST · 2024 · Rajanpur, Punjab

Connecting Resilience

Portable shelter solutions and community needs in flood-prone regions

Programme
Transitional shelter, clusters, masterplan
Method
Household survey · community input · precedents
Supervisor
Ar. Kashmala Imtiaz

After the 2022 floods, many families left relief camps and returned to damaged villages. This thesis asks why, and designs a transitional shelter that households can build themselves with local skills and materials: brick, mud plaster, timber and thatch. It is planned to grow from an emergency unit into a permanent home, with space for a kitchen, veranda and cattle, and units that group into community clusters.

    M.Arch studio · NUST · 2023 · I-10 Markaz, Islamabad

    Gentle Density

    Mixed-use gentle-density housing for factory workers

    Site
    I-10 Markaz industrial area
    Modules
    Single · shared · couple · family apartments
    Sheets
    4 × A0
    Course
    Arch-801 · Advanced Architectural Design Studio I
    Studio coordinator
    Ar./Asst. Prof. Safi Ullah

    Project titleExploring the Feasibility of Gentle Density Development: Factory Worker Housing in I-10 Markaz, Islamabad View post ↗

    How can mixed-use gentle density address the housing and transport problems of factory workers in I-10 Markaz? The project starts from an analysis of how industry and housing meet in the Markaz: plot ratios, house density and street sections. It then proposes a low-rise, high-density block of four apartment modules (35 single, 35 shared, 20 couple, 10 family) around shared courts and terraces.

    Urban heritage · 2020 · Chiniot, Punjab

    Revitalizing Chiniot’s Cultural Spine

    A heritage corridor connecting Umar Hayat Mahal and the Shahi Mosque

    Site area
    approx. 18–25 acres
    Concept
    Urban stitching through heritage
    Programme
    Pedestrian spine · artisan workshops · galleries

    Chiniot’s landmarks stand isolated in a dense, traffic-dominated core. The proposal turns the space between them into a walkable cultural spine. Streets are redesigned for pedestrians, and vacant buildings are reused as woodcraft workshops, galleries and community spaces, so that heritage works as a living system that supports local economies.

    Adaptive reuse · 2019 · Mall Road, Lahore

    Tollinton Market

    Boutique restaurant and heritage hotel

    Site area
    3.5–4 acres
    Concept
    Intimate heritage: living and dining in history
    Strategy
    Minimal intervention in the historic shell

    Tollinton Market began as an exhibition hall and later became a market. The project reuses its large-span hall as human-scaled spaces for dining and staying. Verandahs and shaded thresholds come from Lahore’s colonial architecture, and contemporary insertions add new uses without disturbing the original form.

    B.Arch thesis · University of the Punjab · 2021 · Lahore

    Zooscape

    Immersion in nature: transforming the zoological garden in suburban Lahore

    Type
    Landscape & ecological design
    Focus
    Habitat immersion · visitor routes · site analysis
    Result
    Graduated with distinction

    The thesis reimagines the zoo as a landscape where visitors move through habitats rather than past cages. It combines environmental and ecological design, landscape architecture, site survey and mapping, from the first hand-drawn site plan to the final masterplan.
